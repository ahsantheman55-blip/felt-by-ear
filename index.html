      Voice commands tutorial

      Open the game in your phone browser. Choose Enable voice commands and allow microphone access when asked. Keep the game open in the foreground. The microphone starts again after every announcement, including when sound is playing through a Bluetooth speaker. You do not need to interrupt the game. The game stays quiet until it accepts a command, then it says the result, for example “You fold.” An unrecognised command plays a short soft tone, with no words. Say “echo commands” if you want to hear what was recognised. Say “version” to check you have version 7.

      1. Say “practice commands”, then try “my cards”, “call”, or “raise to two hundred”. The game reads back what it heard without making a move. Say “end practice” when ready.
      2. Say “take a seat”, “start sit and go”, or “start multi table”. Choose a variant by saying “Texas Holdem”, “fixed limit Holdem”, or “five card draw” before sitting down. Difficulty defaults to Normal, which is meant to be beatable.
      3. On your turn, actions need no prefix: “fold”, “one”, “surrender”, “muck”, “I’m out”, or “ninety-nine” folds; “check”, “knock”, “pass”, “call”, “match”, “same”, or “eleven” checks if it is free and calls otherwise; “raise”, “bump”, “up”, “more”, “raise to” an amount, or “fifty-five” for a minimum raise; and “all in”, “shove”, or “everything”.
      4. Information uses “dealer” first, or “croupier” or “table”: “dealer hand” (your two cards and best made hand), “dealer flop”, “dealer turn”, “dealer river”, “dealer board”, “dealer cost” (to call and the pot), “dealer table” (who is in, stacks, button, whose turn, current bet), and “table” by itself also reads the table; “dealer seen” (important visible cards, for example both aces gone), and “dealer card one” or “dealer card two”. “dealer odds”, “chances”, or “numbers” gives your equity against the players still in. “dealer outs” names the count and the cards that help you. “dealer pot odds” compares the percent you need with your equity and says whether that is good or poor value. “dealer maths” walks through the numbers slowly. “dealer stats” is your session: hands played, pots won, biggest pot, and chip trend. “dealer help” is an emergency read: it names your best hand and calls it strong, middling, or weak. Say “coach on” for turn and river warnings when you are one card from a flush or straight, and when a draw misses. “dealer fold” still folds.
      5. Say “dealer advice”, “help me”, or “tips” for a spoken menu: “Advice on what? Pot, players, odds or decision.” “Pot”, or “money” or “chips”, is the total, each player’s contribution this hand, and each stack remaining. “Players”, or “people” or “opponents”, is how each opponent has been playing tonight. “Odds” is equity, outs and pot odds. “Decision” is a short suggestion with a reason. Say “back”, “previous”, or “up a level” to go up, and “done” to leave the menu. An unrecognised answer stays on the same question with a soft tone, and the menu closes quietly after about 20 seconds.
      6. If a command is mis-heard, say “undo”, “oops”, “wrong”, or “dealer undo” within a few seconds, and only before anyone else acts. After that it is locked and the game says “too late”. The undo window can be set between 2 and 5 seconds, and defaults to 3.
      7. Controls: “pause”, “resume”, “slower”, “faster”, “banter on” or “off”, “commentary on” or “off”, “crowd quiet” or “crowd loud”, “save and quit”, and “records”. After you fold, remaining actions use the same pace as when you are in the hand. The next hand deals itself after a short gap unless you pause. The game never acts for you.
      8. In Draw, say “discard card one” through “discard card five”, then “draw cards”, or “stand pat”.
      9. Hands, weakest to strongest: high card, one pair, two pair, three of a kind, straight, flush, full house, four of a kind, straight flush. Say “voice help” to hear this list again, or “stop listening” to turn off the microphone.

      If the browser microphone stops, choose Enable voice commands again. On iPhone you can also enable Voice Control under Settings, Accessibility, Voice Control, then say “Tap” and the button name.

      Sound credits and licences

      Action sounds are from Kenney Vleugels, Casino Audio, licensed CC0, from kenney.nl and OpenGameArt: shuffle, deal, card shove for a fold, chip handling, chip stacking for a raise, and a lowered die throw as a deep turn cue.

      Crowd loops are short excerpts, encoded as small mono MP3 files for iPhone: “Poker Chips & Walla” by craigsmith, CC0, Freesound 817149, used for the default quiet poker-room ambience; “casino_ambiance_01” by joedeshon, CC BY 4.0, Freesound 118853, used for the lively casino setting; and “Very Busy Bar” by richwise, CC0, Freesound 497421, used for the sports-bar setting. The unrecognised-command tone was generated for this game.

      Crowd noise defaults to a moderate poker-room level, because extra sound can get in the way. Use the crowd slider, or say “crowd quiet” or “crowd loud”.
